Atlantic Cod

Field observations

On good spawning years, juvenile cod found schooling on protected side of the island. Appeared suddenly in mid-June in line with first-of-year hatching papers. By late July, big schools appeared to break up into smaller groups foraging among vegetation. Otherwise generally uncommon. Infrequent bycatch of mackerel fishing trips. Distinguished from juvenile pollock on camera by marbling, broken lateral line; tail and head shape also ID tools in hand/clear footage. Seen in pairs (sometimes with pollock) or in medium-sized schools (50-100 in a frame at a time). Flashes of reflective scales on gills especially visible when feeding. Tended to be lower in water column than pollock, usually plankton or benthic feeding. Slight preference towards sandy habitats, but seen in a variety of sediments and vegetation.

How to read the maps
