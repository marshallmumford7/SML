Atlantic Tomcod

Field observations

Juvenile Atlantic tomcod found in eelgrass bed in front of Newcastle Beach adjacent to Newcastle Common. Tomcod closely resemble small cod but have a rounded tail, filamentous pelvic fins, and small eyes. Small cod have a square or concave tail, the pelvic ray is not as elongate, and the eye is larger.

Where to find them

How to read the maps
  • Remote underwater video site, higher abundance
  • Remote underwater video site, seen infrequently
  • Successful trap or seine effort (for seine species, blue diamonds also mark reliable tern foraging)
  • Successful rod and reel catch
  • Red marks on the dark bathymetry maps show bait-fishing catch locations
