Cunner

Field observations

Reliable, common species anywhere vegetation was present. Bottom-loving and territorial. Caught by traps but not by jigging.

Where to find them

How to read the maps
  • Remote underwater video site, higher abundance
  • Remote underwater video site, seen infrequently
  • Successful trap or seine effort (for seine species, blue diamonds also mark reliable tern foraging)
  • Successful rod and reel catch
  • Red marks on the dark bathymetry maps show bait-fishing catch locations
