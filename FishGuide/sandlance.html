Sandlance

Field observations

Young-of-the-year and juvenile or adult sandlance found in eelgrass bed in front of Newcastle Beach adjacent to Newcastle Common. Slender form and sharply pointed snout, coupled with long dorsal fin (separated from the caudal) and absence of ventral fins. The jaws are toothless and there are no teeth on the roof of the mouth.

Where to find them

How to read the maps
  • Remote underwater video site, higher abundance
  • Remote underwater video site, seen infrequently
  • Successful trap or seine effort (for seine species, blue diamonds also mark reliable tern foraging)
  • Successful rod and reel catch
  • Red marks on the dark bathymetry maps show bait-fishing catch locations
